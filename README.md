## GPS Projects - Work in Progress

Last updated: 26 Aug 2026



### Overview

I am always working on multiple projects relating to GPS technology for the speed sailing community. This repository has been created so that I can share details about all of the projects, and their current status.

My focus can change suddenly, so this page helps me to keep track of everything and share with others. I have recently parked numerous projects to make time for Weymouth Speed Week 2026 and Farrel Cup 2026, and they can be found under "Currently on Ice".
